AI Quality Evaluation Harness

Problem
AI assistants can invent answers, leak data or follow instructions hidden in content, and a status-code assertion catches none of that.
What I built
A versioned synthetic dataset, deterministic checks and Behave scenarios that score a fictional help assistant and compare a new build against a reference build.
Testing strategy
Correctness, grounding, unanswerable questions, prompt injection, privacy, robustness and regression. Known false positives and false negatives are recorded instead of hidden.
Evidence
All 21 Behave scenarios pass. The candidate build has seven planted defects and all seven are reported as regressions. Example reports are in the repository.

AI assistant testing, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026

At Northern Block I tested an AI assistant on a property and credential platform. I designed positive, negative, out-of-scope, privacy and hallucination-focused suites, including checks for unavailable or unverified property facts, and automated chat interactions with Behave and Playwright.

These checks show how a system behaved on defined scenarios. They don't prove an AI system is safe, unbiased or free of hallucinations, and I don't present them that way.
Independent Demonstration

What the evaluation harness checks

  1. 01
    Response correctnessRequired facts are present and contradicted claims are absent (COR-001, COR-002).
  2. 02
    GroundingEvery sentence and number must trace back to the supplied source (GRD-001, GRD-002).
  3. 03
    Unanswerable questionsThe assistant must say it doesn't know instead of inventing a price or a date (UNA-001, UNA-002).
  4. 04
    Prompt injectionAn instruction hidden in an untrusted article must not be followed, as shown by a canary token (INJ-001).
  5. 05
    PrivacySynthetic secrets, emails and phone numbers from a restricted record must never appear (PRV-001, PRV-002).
  6. 06
    RegressionA candidate build is diffed against the reference build, and every pass → fail change is reported.
  7. 07
    Limits of automated judgingA known false positive and a known false negative are kept in the dataset and reported separately.
